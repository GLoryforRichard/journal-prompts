## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Journal Prompts ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our website at journalprompts.org. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

**Effective Date: September 8, 2026**

## Information You Provide

- **Browsing and guest writing:** You can browse prompts and write without an account. Guest entries are saved in your browser's local storage on that device.
- **Accounts:** If you register or sign in, we store account information such as your name, email address, authentication details, and account preferences. If you use Google sign-in, Google provides the information needed to identify your account.
- **Signed-in journals:** Entries you save while signed in are sent to our servers and stored with your account so that you can access them after signing in on another device. Your browser may also keep a local draft to protect unsaved work. Guest drafts are not automatically assigned to an account; you can choose to save them to your account.
- **Subscriptions:** We store subscription status and payment references so we can provide your plan's features. Stripe processes payment details; we do not store your full card number.
- **AI prompts:** When you request an AI-generated prompt, we send your selected mood, writing direction, and optional topic to OpenRouter and its model provider. This feature does not send the text of your journal entries to the AI provider. We record usage to apply daily limits.

## Information Collected Automatically

### Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics, such as:

- Pages visited and time spent on site
- General geographic region (country/city level)
- Browser type and device category
- Referral sources

This information helps us understand how visitors use our site so we can improve the experience. Google Analytics may use cookies and online identifiers to collect this information. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### Google AdSense

We may display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google AdSense uses cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

## Cookies

Our site may use cookies for:

- **Authentication** to keep you signed in and protect your account
- **Analytics cookies** (Google Analytics) to understand site usage
- **Advertising cookies** (Google AdSense) to serve relevant ads

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking authentication cookies prevents account sign-in and cloud saving. Clearing local storage removes guest entries and local drafts on that device.

## Data Storage and Security

- Guest entries and local drafts are stored in your browser. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to access locally stored data. We cannot recover guest entries after that browser data is deleted.
- Signed-in entries are stored in our hosted database, with access checked against your authenticated account. They are not end-to-end encrypted. Network connections use HTTPS.
- We keep account information and saved entries to provide the service. You can delete saved entries from My Journal. To request account deletion or ask about retained data, contact us using the address below.
- Your billing history may also be retained by Stripe under its own policies. Deleting an entry does not cancel a subscription; subscriptions are managed separately through billing settings.

## Third-Party Services

We use service providers to operate the site, including:

- **Cloudflare** for hosting and delivery
- **Neon** for the hosted account and journal database
- **Google** for optional account sign-in and site analytics
- **Stripe** for payments and subscription management
- **OpenRouter and its selected model provider** for AI prompt generation
- Analytics, advertising, and email providers when those services are enabled

These services are governed by their own privacy policies.

## Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
